Methodology for Best Outcomes

  1. Start tiny: Begin with a 2-minute version of the habit to reduce friction.
  2. Use If-Then plans: Define clear cues (time/place) to trigger action automatically.
  3. Track completion, not mood: Log behavior daily to build objective feedback loops.
  4. Review weekly: Keep what works, simplify what fails, and avoid all-or-nothing thinking.
  5. Identity reinforcement: Treat each check-in as evidence of who you are becoming.
